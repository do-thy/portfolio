Achievements

International Mobility Program

I took part in an international mobility program, the International Student Engagement and Exploration Program, at Shenzhen City Polytechnic (Shenzhen Institute of Technology) in Shenzhen, China. There I presented our research on earlier versions of the ARISE app, a campus wayfinding system, and received a Certificate of Appreciation on May 21, 2026.

IoEnnovate Exhibit

IoEnnovate was the first Internet of Things exhibit organized by the BSIT students of St. Dominic College of Asia, held on April 29-30, 2026 under the theme "From Sensors to Systems: The Future Runs on IoT." The event featured live demonstrations of smart devices and technology solutions to real-world problems, and I took part by presenting earlier versions of the ARISE mobile app.

CodeChum National Programming Challenge Finalist

The CodeChum National Programming Challenge is a nationwide competition that tests and showcases the programming skills of students from CodeChum partner schools across the Philippines. I became a finalist as one of ten BSIT students chosen to represent St. Dominic College of Asia in the 2025 season, where our school advanced through the preliminary and semifinal rounds to the Finals Round on November 4, 2025.

Academic Excellence

Consistent hard work in my BSIT studies has earned me academic recognition from St. Dominic College of Asia, including a place on the Dean's List and awards at the college's Students Excellence ceremony. These honors reflect the discipline, curiosity, and steady effort I bring to my coursework.
