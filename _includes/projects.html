ARISE Web Application

Arise is an interactive campus navigation platform that lets visitors explore buildings through immersive 360° panoramas. They can move through indoor spaces, search for rooms and facilities, and get step-by-step directions to any destination, including a Nearest Exit route for emergencies. It works on touchscreen kiosks placed around campus and on phones, with a layout tuned to each. Behind the scenes, an admin dashboard lets staff build and maintain the tour, from the map and room listings to kiosk devices and on-screen signage, with no developer needed. The result is a wayfinding experience that helps newcomers find their way around campus on their own.

ARISE Mobile Application

Arise Mobile is a campus navigation app that lets visitors explore and find their way around a building through immersive 360° panoramic tours. Visitors can search for rooms, browse a directory, and follow turn-by-turn directions that walk them through the space, including a nearest-exit route for emergencies. The app also uses the phone's camera for augmented reality features: scanning a room's placard to identify it instantly, and stepping into a room's 360° view through a virtual doorway. Designed to be simple and welcoming, it needs no sign-up, and visitors can save their favorite rooms for later. It is built with React Native and Expo.

Ngayona Habit

Ngayona Habit is a local, Android-based timer application specifically designed to support task tracking, time-setting, alarms, and reminders. Its primary goal is to assist users, particularly students in managing their time efficiently by providing a user-friendly interface platform for organizing tasks and staying on schedule.
